Pi Open Source (PiOS) — FODUBU Unified Apps

FODUBU is a multisector Utility organization building three Pi-powered decentralized applications for trade, agriculture, and environmental marketplaces.
These apps are built to conduct buying and selling transactions using Pi as the primary payment method.

About FODUBU

FODUBU (Force de Développement et Ubuntu au Burundi) is a Utility Fed, Multisector company global ppowering trade growth and digital marketplaces through Pi Network.
All three DApps are part of the FODUBU Pi ecosystem, enabling secure transactions and payment verification with Pi.
